The Rubrics
Each submission is scored across five criteria worth 10 points each, for a total of 50 points.
1. Problem Understanding & Validation | 10 Points
How well does the participant demonstrate that they understand and have investigated a meaningful problem faced
by students?
Criteria Points
Clearly identifies a specific and meaningful problem faced by students 3
Provides relevant research or evidence supporting the identified problem 3
Demonstrates understanding of which students are affected and their specific
experiences or needs.

2. Solution Quality & Innovation | 10 Points
How effectively does the proposed solution address the identified student problem?
Criteria Points
Solution directly addresses the identified problem 3
Proposed features provide meaningful value to the intended students 3
Features and design decisions are purposeful rather than unnecessarily complex 2
Solution demonstrates a creative or well-considered approach to addressing the
identified problem. (This may include a distinctive approach to the problem, meaningful

3. Student Usability & Accessibility | 10 Points
How easily can the intended student understand, navigate, and accomplish their goals using the platform?
Criteria Points
The solution clearly addresses the needs of students 3
The main features help users accomplish their intended goals 3
The interface and interactions are clear and easy to use 2
The solution considers accessibility, inclusivity, and the needs of students with different
abilities and needs.
2
4. UI Design & Visual Communication | 10 Points
How effectively does the visual design communicate the platform and support the student experience?
Criteria Points
Information is organized clearly and is easy to understand 3
Typography, colours, spacing, icons, and imagery are used effectively 3
The design is consistent across the platform 2
The visual design is appropriate for the intended users and purpose of the platform 2
5. Execution, Functionality & Technical Implementation | 10 Points

as demonstrated across their submitted materials and live website?
Criteria Points
The proposed solution is fully translated into the website. 4
Core features and interactions are working and can be tested by judges through the
published website
